# Challenge 2026: the plan

Generated from the tracker seed. The live version (with your progress) is the Roadmap in the app and the Plan tab in the sheet.

## Daily shape

### Weekday (about 9.5h)

> Suggested order: DSA in the morning while you are fresh, LLD after lunch, the third slot in the evening. Revise first thing or just before bed. Times are yours to choose.
- **Revise** (0.75h): Clear the Revise queue. For each problem, recall the approach in 2 minutes and re-code it if unsure. Say each card answer out loud before revealing it.
- **DSA** (3.5h): This week's DSA topic: 4 new problems. Time-box 40 minutes per medium and 60 per hard, then read the editorial. Log each one with its key idea in one line.
- **LLD** (3h): A course lesson coded in Java, or one design problem: requirements, entities, class diagram, code, then compare with the AlgoMaster solution and note what you missed.
- **Third slot** (2h): This week's CS task (from W07: CS on Mon/Wed/Fri, HLD on Tue/Thu). Read, write 4 to 6 answer cards, and say two answers out loud.
- **Wrap-up** (0.25h): Daily log: hours, summary, energy. Pick tomorrow's top three.

### Saturday (about 9.5h)

- **Revise** (0.75h): Clear the Revise queue.
- **Contest** (2.5h): LeetCode Biweekly at 8:00 PM IST on contest Saturdays; a virtual contest on the other Saturdays. Upsolve what you could not finish.
- **DSA** (1.5h): Re-solve this week's "hint" and "solution" problems without looking.
- **LLD** (3h): One full design problem end to end with tests (from W11, a 90-minute machine-coding round).
- **Third slot** (1.5h): CS or HLD from this week's plan.
- **Wrap-up** (0.25h): Daily log.

### Sunday (about 7.75h)

- **Contest** (2.5h): LeetCode Weekly at 8:00 AM IST, then upsolve.
- **Revise** (0.75h): Clear the Revise queue.
- **Mock** (1.5h): One mock interview (from W04), logged with mistakes and fixes.
- **Weekly review** (1h): Check the week in the app, move slipped tasks to next week, and adjust next week's plan if needed.
- **Catch-up** (2h): Finish whatever slipped in LLD, CS or HLD.
> Keep Sunday evening free. Three months is a long run, and rest helps memory.
